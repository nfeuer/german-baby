⚠️ ICON FILES NEEDED

Please create two icon files in this directory:
- icon-192.png (192x192 pixels)
- icon-512.png (512x512 pixels)

See ICON-SETUP.md for detailed instructions.

The PWA will work without icons, but they're needed for a professional home screen appearance.

Quick fix: Use https://www.pwabuilder.com/imageGenerator to generate icons.
